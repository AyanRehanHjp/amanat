About Amanat Welfare Trust

Who We Are

Amanat Welfare Trust is a charitable organization that has been working for social welfare since 2021. Our goal is to support people who are facing difficulties in their lives and provide help wherever it is needed.

We try to assist people in different areas such as financial support, education, medical needs and other essential help.

Members of the trust contribute a small monthly amount (₹50) which helps us create a support fund. This fund is used to help people who are truly in need.

Anyone can contribute to this cause with as little as ₹10 or any amount they wish. Even the smallest contribution can make a big difference when people come together for a good cause.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help and support people who are in emergency situations and struggling with financial problems, basic home needs, medical difficulties, or any kind of emergency problem. We believe that small efforts from many people can bring a big positive change in society.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a supportive and caring community where no one feels alone in times of need. We want to create a system where people help each other and work together to improve lives.

How You Can Help

You can become part of Amanat Welfare Trust by contributing a small monthly amount ₹50 per month. Even a contribution starting from ₹5 can help us support people who are in need.

Together we can create a stronger community and bring hope to those who need it the most.
